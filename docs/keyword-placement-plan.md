# DotMiracle: keyword placement and review plan

This is an internal editorial plan, not public website copy. It describes the current React site and its 32 canonical pages, including the homepage. The page themes and city priorities below are **unvalidated hypotheses**, not search-volume or ranking claims. No Search Console, Keyword Planner, lead, competitor-ranking or verified client-attribution dataset was used. User-supplied competitor research is a starting point for verification, not a source of proven demand, ranking causes, pricing or a Google algorithm weight.

## Placement principles

- Assign one primary intent per canonical page; support it with related concepts and real questions. These are topic examples, not text to repeat at a prescribed density.
- Use a readable title, one useful H1, an introductory explanation, relevant headings and descriptive internal links. Write meta descriptions for readers; Google may select its own search snippets.
- Keep core service pages India-wide. Kerala city and industry briefs retain their specific context. Keep the Chennai base and genuine remote coverage; do not imply local offices, qualifications, translated delivery or results that have not been verified.
- Existing pages must be useful and differentiated; there is no automatic generation of city × service pages. A city name is not a unique value proposition.
- The free audit covers one public page; broader audits are separately scoped. Contact creates an email draft which the visitor must send. Do not treat that draft as a confirmed enquiry.
- Mindtech's reported search-click snapshot has unspecified dates and unverified underlying analytics. It is not a lead or revenue result. Clearly distinguish external studies from DotMiracle client work.

## Research-gated expansion

Consider Chennai and Bengaluru first, followed by Delhi NCR and Hyderabad, then Mumbai and Pune. This is a **research sequence**, not a claim about measured city search demand or delivery locations. Test Keyword Planner with India/city, language, date range, seed queries, volume ranges and limitations recorded. Compare relevant Search Console query/page data where available and check genuine sales demand and delivery capacity.

Before any new page, document a distinct intent, a business need in that market, actual remote service coverage, original material, approved proof, a useful next action and the page owner. Ensure the intent isn't already served by an existing page. Add a canonical, internal links, sitemap entry and accessible content only after review. No manufactured offices, reviews, client results or language capabilities. Translation requires confirmed language support and genuine demand. Do not publish one city–service combination per keyword.

## Monthly reviewed workflow

1. **Collect:** Export Search Console performance by page and query for India, noting dates, device and any city limitations. Record Keyword Planner location/language and range; treat estimates as estimates. Organic query terms are not reliably available to site scripts for individual visitors.
2. **Select:** Group related queries by intent and map each group to one existing canonical page. Review whether the page actually answers the question. Prioritise relevance and business fit over impressions alone; avoid interpreting small-sample CTR or rank as causal evidence.
3. **Draft:** Propose specific title/H1/intro, section or internal-link edits, along with a rationale, baseline period, owner and review date. Do not rewrite copy automatically or use keyword density targets.
4. **Approve and publish:** Review accuracy, overlap, claims, accessibility and scope. Update `content/library.json` and, where relevant, `shared/search-pages.js`; run `npm run build` and commit generated `public/` under the existing Cloudflare Pages workflow. Keep canonical URLs and schema consistent with visible content.
5. **Review:** After sufficient data accrues, compare a documented period with a comparable prior period, watching seasonality and other changes. Review search impressions, clicks and page/query mix separately from confirmed, qualified enquiries. Rankings and traffic can change for reasons unrelated to an edit; do not promise results.

The current `generate_lead` data-layer event is emitted when an email draft is prepared, not when it is sent or received. Verify GTM firing, consent, and real inbox/CRM outcomes independently before using any conversion metric. Never include form answers in analytics. No keyword or form changes should be deployed without the normal release review.
