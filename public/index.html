PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Which SEO service should a Kerala business start with?+

Start with the customer action you need. A clinic seeking appointments may need local SEO; a store whose products are difficult to find may need technical or ecommerce SEO. If the obstacle is unclear, request a review of one important public page before committing to a larger project.

Do you work outside Kerala?+

Yes. The studio is based in Chennai, works across Kerala, and can collaborate remotely with businesses elsewhere in India and overseas. We agree on a market you can serve, the audience’s language, and how enquiries will be handled before recommending regional pages.

What is included in the free SEO audit?+

Share one public page, your intended audience, and the action you want visitors to take. The initial review considers search intent, a practical obstacle, and a priority improvement. A site-wide audit, content production, or ongoing campaign is separately scoped.

Can you guarantee rankings or a specific number of leads?+

No. We can agree on deliverables, check implementation, and measure qualified enquiries against a baseline. Search positions and demand change, and another business’s published results are not a forecast for yours.
