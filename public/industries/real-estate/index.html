DOTMIRACLE / FIELD SERIES

Separate a local area guide from a live listing

Someone exploring a neighbourhood needs context; a buyer comparing two available homes needs verified property details. We map area discovery, project comparison, and viewing requests to the right pages. This avoids repeating the same listings under dozens of near-identical location headings.

Earn trust with checkable project facts

Publish owner-approved photographs, current availability, location details, and the information a buyer needs before a visit. Keep publication dates and the responsible contact clear. Have the responsible professional verify statutory disclosures and any registration claims; do not claim approvals, investment returns, or completion dates without evidence.

Track the journey beyond the listing

Follow an organic entry page through to a specific enquiry and, when the sales team confirms it, an attended site visit. Update pages when stock, prices, or access changes. Compare properties with similar availability and buyer intent rather than attributing all changes in demand to SEO.

PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What should happen when a property is sold or unavailable?+

Update its status promptly. If the page still helps visitors understand a project, keep the status clear and offer genuinely comparable options. Redirect only when there is a suitable replacement, not automatically to the homepage.

Is a site-visit form submission a qualified lead?+

Not by itself. Confirm the visitor’s requirements, the available property, and whether a viewing was arranged. Report submitted forms, qualified conversations, and attended visits as separate outcomes.
