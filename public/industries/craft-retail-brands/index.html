Build collections around a buying decision

For a Kerala handloom or homeware shop, an informative collection is more useful than another generic craft article. Identify whether a visitor is choosing a material, use, size, or gift. Connect that collection to items the shop can actually supply. Keep retail buying guides separate from bulk-order information.

Make provenance useful and verifiable

Describe the maker, material, process, care instructions, dimensions, and natural variation where those facts can be verified. Publish original photographs with permission. Do not claim a certification, origin, or artisan partnership you cannot document. Practical product details help a buyer decide without relying on promotional language.

Protect useful pages when stock changes

For variations of one item, explain what changes and which version is available. Distinguish a temporary stockout from a permanently retired product. Check the links and alternatives in either case, rather than sending every unavailable product to the homepage. Only state dispatch times and destinations the business can support.

Measure orders, not just product views

Test a mobile shopper’s path from collection to product to checkout. With the owner’s authorised shop data, separate discovery, completed orders, returns, and wholesale requests. If the shop cannot deliver to a visitor’s country, measuring that visit as a sales opportunity will overstate the value of the traffic.

PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How should we describe handmade variations?+

Explain verified material, dimensions, care, and natural differences between items. Use your own photographs or images with permission, and distinguish a representative sample from the exact item for sale.

Should a temporarily sold-out piece be redirected?+

Not automatically. If it will return, communicate availability honestly and offer relevant alternatives. For a permanently discontinued item, choose a suitable alternative only when it genuinely serves the same intent.
